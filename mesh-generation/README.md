# Mesh Generation Research Watch

This research stream tracks academic advances in mesh generation for engineering simulation, with emphasis on CAE, CFD, FEM, computational geometry, and scientific computing.

## Scope

Priority topics include:

- automated mesh generation;
- adaptive and anisotropic meshing;
- differentiable meshing;
- solver-in-the-loop mesh optimization;
- reinforcement learning and self-play for meshing;
- Transformer-based methods;
- graph neural networks;
- topology-aware meshing;
- geometry-aware learned representations;
- hybrid neural-classical pipelines;
- mesh quality, robustness, and validity;
- generalization across geometries and simulation regimes.

## Research objective

The goal is to identify methods that materially improve the reliability, automation, quality, efficiency, or generality of meshing workflows used in engineering simulation.

Reports should distinguish between incremental benchmark improvements and deeper changes in how meshing is formulated, learned, optimized, or coupled to downstream solvers.

## Output

Dated reports should focus on genuinely relevant new work and capture:

- title, authors or institution, date, and publication status;
- direct primary-source link;
- the meshing problem being addressed;
- methodological contribution;
- evidence and evaluation setup;
- mesh-quality or downstream-solver metrics when available;
- limitations, assumptions, and failure modes;
- whether the contribution is incremental or conceptually significant;
- relevance to practical CAE, CFD, FEM, or scientific-simulation pipelines.

If no meaningful advances appear in a given week, the report should state that briefly rather than include low-value filler.
